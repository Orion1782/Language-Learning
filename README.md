# Language Learning Principles.
I'm going to share my experiences of self-studying and mastering English and Cantonese as my second and third languages here. But first, let us start with my fundemental principles for language learning. I hope it helps.

## Principal of Learning Language

- Immersive exposure: practical communication without being professional;
- "Fake it until make it": prioritizing real-world usage over rote memorization;
- Long-term Practice: take language learning as a means to end, utilize it for other stuff.

## What is "Language"?

In _structural linguistics_, a language is a system of signs and rules that allows members of a community to communicate meaningfully, enabling communication and the transmission of meaning within a community.
